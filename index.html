I. The Gospel

From the incense and the amplifier hum comes The Heavy Haze: a slow-burning liturgy of fuzz, tolling riffs and smoke thick enough to kneel in. Down-tuned strings drag like censers on chains; the drums fall like cathedral bells counting the hours of a long night.

Born of stained glass and stoned basements, of Latin half-remembered and records worn grey, every song is a procession — patient, heavy, and reverent toward the riff above all.

“Lower the tuning. Raise the smoke. Let the riff be thy rosary.” — Liber Fumi, iii:7
